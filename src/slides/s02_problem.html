The idea · 02

The price keeps moving.
Nobody looks again.

A hotel room is priced like an airline seat — it changes daily. But a booking is treated as final the moment it is paid.

One room, one price, forty-two days

The room was refundable the whole time. Rebooking on day 31 and cancelling the first booking would have returned ₹1,400 — the guest never knew.

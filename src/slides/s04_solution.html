The idea · 04

Add it once. We do the looking.

No account linking, no new booking habit. The guest keeps booking wherever they already book.

1

Add the booking

Forward the confirmation email, send a screenshot, paste a link, or message it on WhatsApp. Pay the trip fee by UPI.

About 20 seconds
2

We watch it daily

The same hotel, the same dates, the same room type — rechecked every day until the deadline.

Roughly 20 checks
3

We alert on a drop

WhatsApp, push or email the moment a cheaper price appears, with the deadline shown.

Within the day
4

Rebook and save

Guided steps: book the cheaper room first, check it, then cancel the old one. The saving is recorded.

The guest keeps all of it
We never touch the guest's account or card. Every booking and cancellation stays in the app they already use — we only tell them when, and point them at the cheaper room.
Tracking stops automatically at the free-cancellation deadline, after which a drop is of no use

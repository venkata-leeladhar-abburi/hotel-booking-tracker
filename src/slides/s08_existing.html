Market · 08

Six people already do a piece of this.

None of them does all of it in India, and the closest one launched three weeks ago.

Onto

Bengaluru

Launched 22 Oct 2026 with $1M pre-seed. Refunds the drop to the original payment method, including bookings made elsewhere. Says 1 in 2 bookings got a refund.1

The direct competitor

Pruvo

Israel

Founded 2016, $1.1M seed. Forward the email to an address, free to the traveller; earns through B2B rebooking for agencies.2

Free · the pricing problem

DreamCheaper

Berlin

Cancels and rebooks automatically, and charges 20% of the saving. Automatic rebooking is where we could grow; a cut of the saving is not our model.3

Automatic rebooking

HotelSlash

United States

A $29.95 a year membership. Tracks and alerts; the traveller rebooks. A yearly fee is a bigger ask in India than one small fee per trip.4

Subscription

Google Hotels

Global

Free price-drop email alerts, but per property and before you book — it does not know what you paid or when you must cancel by.5

Pre-booking only

MakeMyTrip

India

Price Drop Protection exists — but it is flights only, ₹229 domestic and ₹349 international per person, and only on their own bookings.6

No hotel equivalent

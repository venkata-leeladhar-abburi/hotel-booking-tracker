Getting the first hundred.

One number does the selling: the rupees a real person got back. Everything below is a way of showing it.

First 100 users

Hand-picked, by hand

Family, college, hostel and local travel WhatsApp groups. We add their bookings for them, by hand if we must.

Timing

Launch into a peak

December–January, Sankranti, summer, pilgrimage seasons, long weekends, weddings — people book early for all of them.

Content

Reels, in three languages

"I saved ₹2,700 after I had already booked" in Telugu, Hindi and English. The alert, then the refund. Thirty seconds.

Distribution

Micro-creators

Regional travel accounts, 10–100k followers, paid per post. Cheaper than ads and more trusted for a money claim.

Compounding

SEO pages

"Price dropped after booking — can I rebook?" per city and per site. Slow, but it pays after the spend stops.

Loop

Referral on the saving

The moment someone is told they saved is the moment they share. Put the referral there, nowhere else.

The goal of the first hundred is not revenue. It is thirty screenshots of real savings — without those, none of the channels above work.

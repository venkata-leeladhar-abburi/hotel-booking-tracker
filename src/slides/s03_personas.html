The idea · 03

Four kinds of traveller,
one shared habit.

They book a refundable room weeks early to lock it in — then never look at the price again.

Families on holiday

Book early for school holidays, often two rooms, and rarely recheck.

Pilgrims

Fixed dates, booked months ahead, prices swing hard near the season.

Weekend getaways

Grab a room the moment a long weekend appears, then stop looking.

Small business travellers

Pay themselves, claim it back, and gain directly from any drop.

All four book refundable rooms early — the exact window in which about 40% of reservations get cheaper.

Did it right

BuyHatke

Lost

hiQ v LinkedIn

Won on public data, lost on the terms — fake accounts, $500k judgment.3

Allowed

Meta v Bright Data

Logged-out public data did not breach Meta's terms. The line is logging in.4

Still running

Ryanair v Booking.com

Scraping behind a login drew a US jury verdict, overturned in 2025 and under appeal. Unsettled law — so we avoid logins entirely.5

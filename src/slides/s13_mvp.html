What ships in the first eight weeks.

Ten things. Anything not on this list is slide 17's problem.

01

Phone OTP sign-in

No password to forget, no email to verify.

02

Four ways to add

Forwarded email, screenshot, booking link, or WhatsApp message.

03

AI reads the booking

Gemini Flash extracts hotel, dates, room and price — the guest confirms before we start.

04

Hotel matching

Resolve the property to a Google Hotels token so we recheck the same place.

05

Scheduled checks

A daily job per booking, stopping at the cancellation deadline.

06

Drop alerts

WhatsApp template, push and email — whichever the guest chose.

07

Guided rebook

Book the cheaper room first, then cancel. Shown in that order, with the link.

08

Saved-so-far counter

The running total. The reason anyone opens the app twice.

09

Deadline reminders

A nudge before free cancellation expires, drop or no drop.

10

Razorpay UPI

Charged after a saving is confirmed, never before.

Money · 24

We earn when the guest wins.

No subscription. If nothing drops, nothing is owed — which is also the easiest thing to explain in an ad.

Primary

10–15% of the saving

Charged only after a rebooking is confirmed. On a ₹1,000 saving that is ₹100–150, and the guest keeps the rest.

The first 3 bookings are free, so the first alert costs nothing and proves the product before any charge.

Secondary

Affiliate on rebooks

The rebook goes through our link. Agoda pays about 4–5% by volume, Booking.com 4% on completed stays via CJ.1

Earned on the same event as the fee, so a successful saving pays twice.

Small, early

Target-price alert

About ₹49 to watch a hotel before booking and alert at a price the guest names.

Matters because it is the only product that works for non-refundable bookings.

Later — B2B for travel agents

Agents re-price client bookings in bulk and keep the difference. Pruvo's actual business, and it does not need consumer scale.

Unit economics, in one line

About ₹30 to track a booking against about ₹150 earned when one saves — the full derivation is on the next slide.

How we build it · 20

One source now, three in reserve.

Depending on a single price feed is the biggest technical risk in the product.

MVP · chosen

SerpApi

Google Hotels prices by property token. Free 250 searches a month to validate, then $75 for 5,000.1

Risk — one vendor between us and every price.

Backup

MakCorps · Apify

MakCorps covers 200+ sites; Apify has a Google Hotels actor. Swapped in if SerpApi coverage or health drops.2

Why not first — less predictable, and a second bill.

Earns money

Agoda · Booking.com

Affiliate APIs give prices and pay for the rebook: Agoda about 4–5%, Booking.com 4% through CJ.3

Catch — only their own inventory, so coverage is partial.

Later

TBO

India-strong B2B hotel API. The route to owning the booking and refunding automatically — needs agency registration and KYC.4

Blocked on — company, GST, approval.

Do not plan on Amadeus. The Self-Service API portal shut down on 17 July 2026. Any tutorial recommending it is out of date.5

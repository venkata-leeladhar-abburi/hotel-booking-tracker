The idea · 06

Forty-two days, from the guest's side.

One real-shaped booking, end to end. Everything between day 0 and the alert happens without them.

Day 0

Booking added

Forwards the confirmation, confirms what we read, and pays the trip fee by UPI.

Days 1–8

Quiet watching

A daily price check against the same hotel and dates. No messages — silence is the product working.

Day 9

Drop found · alert

₹5,000 seen on Agoda, confirmed by a second check. Alert names the site, the time, and the days left to cancel free.

Day 9

Rebooked

Books the cheaper room first, then cancels the original. Order matters — we show it that way round.

Day 10

Saving recorded

₹1,400 added to their total — all of it theirs. The fee was paid once, on day 0.

Day 40

Tracking ends

Watching stops at the cancellation deadline. Booking data is dropped once there is nothing left to do.

The guest acts twice in forty-two days: once to add the booking, once to rebook. Everything else is ours.

Most trips never drop. The fee has to feel fair to the guests who get nothing back, too.

At ₹150 the guests, as a group, get back roughly what they paid. At ₹50 they get back 2–4 times as much.

Every fee in the range is profitable for us. The question is what the guest feels — keep the fee well below a typical saving, which argues for ₹50–99 on smaller bookings.
Funnel and costs are our estimates, not measurements. Assumes a ₹1,000–1,400 average saving, a ₹5,000 average rebooking, and half of rebooks through an Agoda or Booking.com link at 4%.

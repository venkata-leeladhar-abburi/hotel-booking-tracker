What this becomes if it works.

Each step removes work from the guest. The end state is that they never have to act at all.

01

Auto cancel and rebook

We do both sides and the difference comes back automatically. Needs the booking to be ours — which is what TBO unlocks.

02

Book with us

Price protection built into the booking itself. The guest stops thinking about rebooking because we already handle it.

03

Price prediction

Enough tracked bookings become a dataset: "this hotel usually drops 12 days out — wait." Advice before the booking, not after.

04

Beyond hotels

Buses are refundable and repriced constantly. Then homestays and packages. The same loop, different inventory.

05

Travel-agent dashboard

An agent re-prices a hundred client bookings at once. Fewer customers, far larger each, no consumer marketing spend.

06

Trip assistant in WhatsApp

Task-specific only — rebooking, deadlines, prices. A general assistant would break WhatsApp policy, so it stays narrow.1

The through-line: today we tell the guest, tomorrow we do it for them, eventually they book with us and it never comes up.

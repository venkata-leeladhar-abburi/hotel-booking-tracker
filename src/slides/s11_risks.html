Six things that could sink this.

Written down plainly, with what we would actually do about each. None of them is solved yet.

01

A free competitor

Pruvo is free and Onto refunds the whole drop. Charging 10–15% looks worse side by side.

Mitigation — first 3 bookings free; we charge only against a real saving.

02

We cannot see every price

Coupons, bank offers and cashback never reach the price feed, so a "drop" may not beat what was actually paid.

Mitigation — compare against the amount paid; alert only above a margin.

03

Matching the right room

"Deluxe Double" on one site is "Superior King" on another. A wrong match is a false alert.

Mitigation — match on property token plus dates; show what we compared.

04

Only free-cancellation bookings

A non-refundable booking cannot be rebooked, so a drop is worthless — ruling out a real share of the market.

Mitigation — say so at sign-up; push the pre-booking target-price alert.

05

Booking sites may push back

We send cancellations their way. A platform could block affiliate links or change terms.

Mitigation — licensed APIs only, affiliate links for rebooks, never scrape behind a login.

06

Trust

We ask a stranger to forward their name, dates and what they paid. A lot for a new brand.

Mitigation — never ask for passwords or OTPs; delete on request, stated up front.

Mitigations are proposals for discussion, not decisions already taken

Where the prices come from.

The booking sites already publish their prices to Google. We read Google, through a licensed API — we never scrape a booking site.

1

Booking sites publish

MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com and Agoda send their rates to Google Hotels themselves.1

2

SerpApi reads Google

A property token returns featured prices and the price per site, by room.2

3

We compare

Same hotel, same dates, against the amount the guest actually paid.

4

We alert

Only when the gap clears a margin and the deadline has not passed.

Why this works in India

MakeMyTrip appears in 52% of sponsored and over 70% of organic Google Hotels listings in India.3 Reading Google reaches the dominant seller without touching its site.

It is also the legal position we want: a licensed API, public data, no login, no fake accounts.

What it cannot see

Coupons, bank offers and wallet cashback are invisible. Some small and OYO-only properties are missing. We get the cheapest room per site, not every room type.

Validation gate — before any code: run 20 real hotels through the free 250 searches and measure how many match. If coverage is poor, the idea stops here.

SerpApi returns the cheapest price per site — often a different room or a non-refundable rate. Sent unfiltered, about 1 in 3 alerts would be wrong or unusable.

With the safeguards on slide 16 90–95% of alerts correct — under 5–10% false. Every alert is checked twice and only sent when the room and rate match.

Never 100%

Coupons, bank offers, member prices and cached prices never reach us. That is why every alert says book first, check, then cancel.

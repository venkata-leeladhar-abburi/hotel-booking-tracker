Product · 16

Safe with no support team.

There is no support staff at launch. So the product has to stay safe even when an alert is wrong.

The rule on every alert

Book the new room first. Check it. Then cancel the old one.

A wrong alert then costs the guest a few minutes — never money, never their room. This protects us more than anything else.

Support target 1–3 issues per 100 trips, almost all closed by the buttons on the alert.
01

Check twice

Re-check the price just before sending. Alert only if the second check agrees.

02

Real drops only

At least ₹500 and at least 5% below what the guest paid.

03

Same room, or silence

Same site, same room name, refundable rate. Not sure? Don't send.

04

Careful words

"Price seen at ₹5,000 on Agoda at 3:10 pm" — never "You will save ₹1,400".

05

Buttons, not tickets

Wrong hotel · Price not there · Stop tracking. Each fixes itself and teaches the matcher.

06

One-tap deletion

The trip and its data go in one tap. We never ask for a card number, OTP or password.

Guidance to finalise before the beta — not fixed policy. Deletion duties under the DPDP Act are on slide 23.

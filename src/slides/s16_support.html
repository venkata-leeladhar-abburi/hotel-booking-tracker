When it goes wrong, which it will.

A draft to argue with, not policy. Deadlines make support urgent in a way most apps never face.

Response targets

Anything with a deadline inside 48 hours comes first.

WhatsApp under 2 hours in waking hours · everything else same day.

The hard rule

We never ask for a card number, an OTP or a booking-site password. Support staff cannot ask either.

Escalation and learning

Deadline risk goes to the founder on call. Every case is logged so the matcher improves.
